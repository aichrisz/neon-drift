# Changelog

## 1.0.0 local release — 2026-10-22

- Playable rear-camera perspective traffic arcade with procedural rainy skyline and wet road.
- Deterministic engine, held steering, brake priority, consumable nitro, swept contact crash, narrow-pass rewards, gradual capped difficulty.
- Native responsive controls, pause/resume/lifecycle handling, reduced decoration and validated session/local records.
- Classic zero-dependency runtime, allowlist loopback server, build, standalone HTML exporter and tracked-source ZIP helper.
- Parent acceptance: 19 unit/boundary tests, 7 browser tests and 13 additional edge cases passed; source/build/standalone and emulated multitouch were exercised independently.
- Corrected the permanent inter-lane safe corridor, enabled-control focus order and persistent in-play heading after observed regressions; online dependency audit returned zero findings.
- Fresh independent Astra review approved local delivery. Chromium automation only; physical devices and other browser engines remain unverified. No public deployment.
